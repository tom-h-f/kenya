The result that constrains the others

A ranking that does not survive being tested

The detector ranks accounts by centrality. To test whether that ranking is real, we collected the full posting history of a random 80% of the accounts it ranked and left the other 20% untouched, then ranked again.

Accounts that were given history left the ranking entirely. The untouched control, drawn from the same list by the same rule, largely stayed. The ranking was measuring how little we knew about an account, not how coordinated it was.
